Building My Website Like It's 2006

Posted: 6th March 2026

Modern websites all look the same. Clean, minimal and corporate.

But I miss when the internet felt personal. Everyone had their own strange layouts, music players and ridiculous colour schemes.

So I decided to rebuild my website in the style of an old MySpace page. No frameworks, no fancy tools — just HTML and CSS.

Why?

Because the internet used to feel like people's bedrooms. Now it feels more like a shopping mall.
